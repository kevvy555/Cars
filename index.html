02 / UNDER THE BONNET

What goes into the number?

Each monthly figure is a five-year total divided by 60. It's the cost of ownership, not the cost of financing a car.

01

Depreciation

Representative 2026 purchase value minus an estimated 2031 resale value. Forecasts are uncertain and not guaranteed.

02

Running costs

Fuel at the chosen annual mileage, current-rate road tax, estimated insurance and independent-specialist servicing.

03

Wear & risk

Tyres, brake/repair reserves, MOTs and preparation costs. Reserves are prudent allowances, not spending commitments.

04

Performance & fit

Power-to-weight uses representative PS and kg. 0–60 times and handling ratings are approximate. Small rear seats aren't full adult accommodation.

Important context. The model was prepared in November 2026 using indicative UK values, representative variants and assumptions from our comparison. It is not a live market feed. Boot volumes can vary by equipment and measuring method; some sports cars split luggage capacity across two compartments. Manufacturer/owner performance results can differ. The Peugeot 306 Rallye assumes a finished restoration, and the 205 GTi is a standard road-car proxy, not a valuation of a specific competition vehicle. The unfinished Mini projects are deliberately excluded.
